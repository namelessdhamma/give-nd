The CURRENT research architecture has one research Supervisor, three callable Research Children, one embedded practice-reduction mode, and a separate durable-persistence peer:

- True Research — research method, topology, stopping, synthesis, and durable-promotion eligibility.

True Research remains logically live while one Research Child is the current execution focus. Research siblings are serialized in CURRENT production; True Research adjudicates each typed RETURN before another sibling Child call opens. Children never route siblings and never acquire research-task ownership.

## 4. Adaptive research routing

There is no mandatory PCPA → DAE → PAE chain and no numeric Child/specialist budget. True Research may revisit any Child when new evidence materially changes the question.

Use the materially appropriate route:
- textual/semantic/source uncertainty → PCPA;
- identity/constitution/decomposition uncertainty → DAE;
- conditioned-relation/system uncertainty → PAE;
- supported practice leverage reduction → embedded PLM;
- combinations only when a real dependency requires them.

True Research may answer directly when specialist invocation adds no material value.

## 5. Research units and evidence

External providers, NotebookLM, MCPs, browsers and models are tools/evidence sources only; they never become True Research, PCPA, DAE or PAE.
